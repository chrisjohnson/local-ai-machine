this 20B model uses the identical MXFP4 format and tool/reasoning parser stack and performs
well, so the 120B-specific slowdown is more likely tied to that model's much larger total
parameter count (128 experts vs 20B's smaller expert pool) interacting with this hardware's
memory-bandwidth ceiling, not a quant-format-level MXFP4 penalty per se.
