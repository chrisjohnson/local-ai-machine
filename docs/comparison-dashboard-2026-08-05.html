Biggest findings

  • Fastest token generation across every small build: laguna-xs-2.1-33b via llama.cpp direct at 95.1 tok/s (tg128) — MoE-class, ~15% ahead of the next-fastest (GLM-4.7-Flash llama.cpp, 71.0). For a comparison of standing-role engineering value, GLM-4.7-Flash at 71.0 tok/s with only 17,484 MiB GTT is the strongest speed-per-memory ratio in the sweep.
  • Smallest memory footprint: qwen3.5-4b vLLM at 23,389 MiB GTT (@0.20 util) — but among the useful-size builds, qwen2.5-vl-7b-instruct at 22.8 GiB GTT (@0.20) and the llama.cpp GGUF family (16-21 GiB GTT) are the practical leaders. Every llama.cpp build fits in under ~21 GiB GTT, roughly 1/5 the footprint of a vLLM 0.90-util build.
  • MTP speculative decoding measured on 4 builds this sweep: qwen3.6-27b dense is the standout at 2.07x (26.38 vs 12.77 tok/s); the three MoE builds gain far less (Ornith 1.17x, qwen3.6-35b-a3b 1.16x, KAT-Coder ~1.08x). Dense models pay off most — MTP draft heads cost little extra compute there.
  • vLLM 0.90-util builds are full-box allocations and must never co-run — glm-4.7-flash-awq, gpt-oss-20b, gemma-4-26b-a4b-it, gemma-4-31b-it, and qwen3.6-27b all peak at 115-120 GiB GTT (system 123-127 GB, spilling into swap). Two stacked = the 2026-08-05 OOM thrash. qwen2.5-vl-7b (@0.20) and qwen3.5-4b (@0.20) are the only sub-30GB vLLM builds.
  • Ollama trails llama.cpp direct on every shared model — GLM-4.7-Flash 59.1 vs 71.0 tok/s, qwen3.6-27b 10.46 vs 12.8, qwen3.6-35b-a3b 40.7 vs 63.7. Consistent with the prior dashboard's finding that Ollama's serving overhead costs MoE models proportionally more.

Data quality — an honest accounting

  • Every vLLM build now has memory-capture data — all 8 vLLM small builds have vllm-memory-capture-v1 (GTT + system peak, weights) on top of fresh vllm-speed-c1c8-v2 speed data. Speed data dates 2026-07-24/25 (same kernel 6.18.39, Mesa 25.3.6) — explicitly not re-run per M-083 scope; memory captured fresh 2026-08-05.
  • All llama.cpp + ollama speed/memory fresh 2026-08-05, one model at a time, download queue paused, container verified stopped between runs.
  • Ornith-1.0-35B-MTP Q8_0 benchmarked per M-083 directive (download completed mid-sweep) — plain + MTP + memory. First attempt corrupted by the docker-compose-app unit bringing up the full stack on download-complete (base_used 118,931 MiB); discarded and re-run clean at 4.5GB base.
  • gemma-4-26b--ollama stays BROKEN — gemma4 arch unsupported in Ollama 0.17.7 (pinned; upgrade is a hard-stop decision). No data fabricated.
  • north-mini-code-1.0-w4a16 stays BROKEN — unresolved --max-model-len placeholder; no memory capture attempted.
  • Historical context: prior passes measured slightly different numbers on some shared builds (e.g. GLM-4.7-Flash llama.cpp 71.24 → 71.0; Qwen3.6-27B llama.cpp 12.72/12.77 → 12.8; Ollama GLM 61.98 → 59.1, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 41.27 → 40.7). Within run-to-run noise (pp512 especially is load-sensitive); the newer 2026-08-05 figures are the benchmark-of-record in the build files.
  • The 2026-08-05 OOM incident is fully documented in catalog/builds/qwen3.6-27b--vllm-therock-gfx1151.yaml and the M-083 card: two stacked 0.90-util vLLM builds (qwen3.6-27b + glm-4.7-flash-awq) exceeded the 124GB pool → swap thrash → power cycle. /tmp/vllm_mem.sh hardened (pre-flight guard + guaranteed teardown trap); the 0.90-util co-run warning is recorded in every affected build file.
